About MS Mobiles

MS Mobiles provides professional mobile phone repair and service solutions. We help customers repair their smartphones quickly and reliably.


Our services include screen replacement, battery replacement, charging repair, software service, hardware repair and other mobile-related problems.


We also provide quality mobile accessories including chargers, cables, covers, tempered glass, earphones, power banks and mobile holders.


Our goal is to provide quality service, transparent repair information and customer satisfaction.

Frequently Asked Questions

Common questions about mobile repair services at MS Mobiles.

What mobile repair services are available in Bengaluru?

MS Mobiles provides mobile repair services including screen replacement, battery replacement, charging repair, speaker and microphone repair, software service, water-damage repair, camera repair and other mobile repair services.

How can I track my mobile repair?

You can track your mobile repair using your Repair ID. Open the Track Repair page, enter your Repair ID, and check your current repair status.

How much does mobile repair cost?

Mobile repair costs depend on the type of problem and the mobile model. MS Mobiles provides an estimated repair cost after checking the device.

What mobile brands does MS Mobiles support?

MS Mobiles provides repair services for popular brands including Apple, Samsung, OnePlus, Xiaomi, Vivo, OPPO, Realme and Motorola.

How do I book a mobile repair?

You can book a mobile repair through the Book Repair form on the MS Mobiles website. Enter your customer details, mobile brand and model, repair problem, preferred date and problem description.
